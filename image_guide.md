## Tips

- **Format**: JPG, **300×300 px** minimum (square). The app crops with `BoxFit.cover`.
- **Style**: Clean, simple, real-life photos or flat illustrations. Avoid cluttered backgrounds.
- **Kid-friendly**: This app targets children learning Mandarin — keep images bright, clear, and appropriate.
- **Licensing**: Use royalty-free sources like [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), [Pexels](https://pexels.com), or [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com).
- **Naming**: File names must match exactly as listed above (lowercase, `.jpg` extension).
